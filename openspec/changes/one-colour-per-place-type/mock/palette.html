one-colour-per-place-type · tasks 1.1 and 1.8

Palette Bench

Eight place types, eight colours. Five already exist. This page is where the other three get decided — nature, place and culture — against the real basemap values, the real teardrop, the real glyphs, and the fourteen markers of the Kyoto trip this product was first built against. Change the three candidates in the rail; every check below re-measures. Contrast ratio is not the instrument at the dark end of the range, so each check reports CIE L* separation as well.

Eight is at the cap, not under it. The first pass stopped at seven and deliberately did not establish that eight was fine. Temple is the eighth: it takes the slate, because the recessive value follows the majority and the majority is temples, and culture — castles, museums, galleries — buys the last open span on the wheel. Check 1.7 is what decides whether that holds.

Values are read from packages/tokens/src/colour.ts and BASEMAP_COLOUR as committed. Pin geometry is the path in apps/web/app/_components/pin.tsx at 32×42 with a 15px glyph at stroke 2.4; glyph outlines are the same Lucide icons both applications resolve. The trip ground is synthetic — no tiles are fetched — but every colour in it is the token the app rewrites the upstream style with, so the adjacencies are real.
